# VoltMapEV 1.15.12 (43)

This mobile release brings the website's newer shared features to iPhone, iPad and Android.

- Improved Discover search, scrolling and access to additional charger results.
- Refreshed colors and station detail presentation.
- Saved charging arrival plans and connector preferences on this device.
- Charger-code entry, charging-network information and support access.
- Charging payment preferences and wallet connection status.

Arrival plans do not hold a connector. Wallet top-ups, refunds, direct payments and remote charging remain unavailable until verified backend services are connected. The app continues to direct users to the charging operator for actual charging and payment. WhatsApp delivery is not claimed as live; App Store builds retain local driver-profile access without a phone number.

## Google Play release notes

Improved charger search, scrolling and expanded results. Refreshed colors, saved arrival plans, charger-code entry, network information and payment preferences. Arrival plans do not reserve a connector. Charging and payment remain with the operator where live services are not connected.
