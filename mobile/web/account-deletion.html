Delete account & data

VoltMapEV privacy choices

Delete inside the app

  1. Open VoltMapEV and select Profile.
  2. Select Delete account & local data.
  3. Review the warning and select Delete permanently.

This immediately removes the profile, locally saved identifier and password hash, favorites, trips, receipts, charger reports, vehicle details, and account preferences stored by VoltMapEV on that device. Repeat on any other device where you have a separate local profile. You can also clear app or browser storage. This does not recall an email you already sent or remove a report you previously posted on another service.

Request help without the app

Email skotla100@gmail.com with the subject “VoltMapEV account deletion request.” Identify the contact address or support conversation whose data you want removed. Do not send passwords, OTPs, payment credentials, Aadhaar, PAN, or banking documents. Support can help remove support messages and explain any service-provider or legally required retention. Local profiles are not synchronized to a central account database, so support cannot remotely erase another device's local storage.

Temporary verification records

When you request optional WhatsApp or email verification, the identity service stores a challenge for up to five minutes and hashed destination/IP rate-limit records for up to one hour. The challenge is deleted after successful verification or exhausted attempts. A returned verified-contact token expires after one day. Deleting a local profile does not recall a delivered message or delete separate Meta, Resend, Cloudflare, or email-provider records. See the privacy policy for details and contact support for help with a provider deletion request.

Future cloud accounts

If synchronized cloud accounts are introduced, this page and the in-app flow will be updated to delete server-side account data and request deletion from applicable service providers.
