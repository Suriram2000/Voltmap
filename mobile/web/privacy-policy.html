Privacy Policy

Effective 13 August 2026

Current release: VoltMapEV is a charger-discovery and trip-planning app. Payments, phone OTP, and receipt delivery are clearly labeled demonstrations until approved production providers are connected.

Information handled by the app

When you choose to create a local profile, VoltMapEV stores your name, email address or phone identifier, a salted password hash, vehicle details, favorites, saved trips, charging demonstration receipts, charger reports, and preferences on your device. Raw passwords, full card details, CVV values, UPI PINs, and real payment credentials are not stored.

When you choose Use current location, the app requests location permission and uses your coordinates to find a nearby Indian area and charging stations. You can deny permission and search manually.

External services

  • Location searches and reverse geocoding may be sent to the Photon/Komoot service, which uses OpenStreetMap place data.
  • The current charging-station inventory is bundled from dated Bureau of Energy Efficiency records. A timestamped operator feed is used only when a production endpoint is configured.
  • Google Maps opens only when you choose verification or directions.
  • A charger report stays on your device unless you choose to open a private, pre-addressed email to the VoltMapEV administrator. VoltMapEV does not send that email itself.

These services may process IP addresses, device information, search terms, or coordinates under their own privacy terms. VoltMapEV does not use advertising trackers or sell personal information in the current release.

Purpose and retention

Data is used to provide charger search, trip planning, saved items, local profiles, support, fraud prevention, and payment demonstrations. Local data remains on your device until you delete it, clear app/browser storage, or uninstall the app. If you choose to send a private report by email, your email provider and the recipient control that message’s retention.

Your choices

You can search and plan trips without an account, deny location permission, remove favorites or trips, and delete the local account and its data from Profile → Delete account & local data. See the account deletion page for another deletion pathway.

Security and children

We use reasonable safeguards and minimize stored payment information, but no device or internet service is completely secure. VoltMapEV is intended for drivers and is not directed to children under 13.

Production service changes

Before real authentication, payment, email, SMS, analytics, or cloud storage is enabled, this policy and the applicable app-store disclosures must be updated to identify the providers, purposes, retention, and user controls.
