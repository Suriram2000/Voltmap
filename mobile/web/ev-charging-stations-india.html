Public EV charging coverage in India

The Government of India reported 29,277 installed public EV charging stations in a Rajya Sabha answer dated 1 August 2025. Karnataka, Maharashtra, Uttar Pradesh, Delhi, Tamil Nadu, Rajasthan, Kerala, Gujarat, Madhya Pradesh, and Telangana were the ten largest state totals in that answer.

How to search for a charger

Search by PIN code or local area

Enter a six-digit PIN code, landmark, neighbourhood, city, or state in VoltMapEV. Detailed bundled results are clearly labelled as demonstrations; the live-search option opens current external map results so you can verify the station before travelling.

Check the details that affect your vehicle

Confirm connector type, charging power, operator, access hours, recent availability, price, and directions. Charger availability and tariffs change, so verify them with the operator or a current map listing before starting a long trip.

How to plan an EV route

Choose the starting point and destination, review the estimated distance and energy, and inspect chargers near the route. For a journey such as Hyderabad to Bengaluru, VoltMapEV filters the bundled candidates by proximity to the route instead of listing every station in India. Always open live directions and confirm working chargers before departure.

Payment safety

The current VoltMapEV checkout is a visible sandbox: it accepts only published test credentials and never moves real money. Production UPI, card, PhonePe, Paytm, and supported wallet payments will remain disabled until a licensed payment gateway, server-side order creation, signature verification, authenticated webhooks, reconciliation, refunds, and merchant approval are configured.

Data transparency: the national total is a dated government aggregate. Individual bundled stations, connector availability, prices, route stops, and checkout sessions are demonstration data, not a complete real-time national feed.
